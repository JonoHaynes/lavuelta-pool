House rules

How the pool works

1Your squad

Every manager drafts one team of eight riders from the start list before Stage 1.

  • You have a budget of 100 credits. Each rider has a value on the Riders page; your eight picks must total 100 or less.
  • Riders can be owned by more than one manager — pick the squad you believe in.
  • Rider values are lower than in the Tour pool, so 100 credits stretch further — you can afford two or even three genuine leaders alongside your stage-hunters.

2Locked for the Vuelta

This is the key rule that keeps it honest:

  • Teams are locked for the entire race — the same eight riders score on all 21 stages.
  • No transfers, no substitutions, no per-stage changes. What you draft is what you ride to Granada.
  • If one of your riders abandons, you simply lose their future points. Choose durable riders.

3Your captain scores double

  • Every stage has a captain — one rider from your squad whose points that day count double. Stage finish points, jersey bonuses and the combativity award all double.
  • Each stage has its own captain. Set all twenty-one in advance when you enter — a climber for the summit finishes, a sprinter for the flat days, a time trialist for Monaco and Jerez.
  • You do not have to change anything day to day. Your plan stands until you change it.
  • Change any stage, any time, from the Captain page. Deadline: 10:00 CET on the morning of the stage. Anything arriving later applies from the next stage. Stages already raced are locked.
  • Final classification bonuses in Granada are not doubled — the captain applies to stage scoring only.

5Jersey bonuses

Extra points each stage for any of your riders who leads a classification — awarded on the standings, even if another rider is wearing that jersey on the road:

Red · La Roja, overall leader+10
Green · points leader+6
Blue polka-dot · king of the mountains+6
White · best young rider+4

Plus the daily most combative rider — the dorsal amarillo, or yellow number — earns +5.

6Final bonuses in Granada

Through the three weeks your riders collect stage points as the race unfolds. When it finishes in Granada, they earn one last set of bonus points for where they place in each of the four classifications. You receive these for every rider in your squad who lands in a position below — added on top of everything they scored during the stages.

7Winning & tie-breaks

  • The manager with the most total points after Stage 21 wins the pool and la roja.
  • Tie-break: most stage wins among owned riders, then the best single-stage points haul.
  • Standings update the moment results are entered — the board you see is always live.

8Prize pool

  • Entry fee: 20 CHF per manager.
  • Deadline: teams must be submitted and the entry fee transferred before the start of Stage 1 (Saturday 22 August, 12:00 CEST).
  • Winner takes all. The whole pot goes to the manager who finishes top of the fantasy classification. There is nothing for second or third.
  • The pot is the total of every entry fee. With nine managers that is 180 CHF, with twelve it is 240 CHF — confirmed once entries close on 22 August.
